The Problem

Bitcoin positions lock everything together

A productive BTC position bundles principal, governance votes, and fee entitlements into one inseparable unit. Today, unlocking liquidity means selling BTC or taking on debt.

Choice Liquidity Now Keep BTC No Debt No Liquidation Risk
Sell BTC Yes No Yes Yes
Borrow MUSD Yes Yes No No
Hold & wait No Yes Yes Yes
Epoch Yes Yes Yes Yes

Epoch's trade-off: the seller gives up uncertain future fee income in exchange for certain MUSD today. No debt. No liquidation. No principal sale.

The Mechanism

Revenue rights as a tradeable primitive

Epoch separates two independent economic exposures from one position:

Principal Exposure

Ownership and future return of BTC locked in the underlying position. The seller always retains this.

Revenue Exposure

Entitlement to fees produced by the position in a specific future epoch. This is what gets traded.

Safety Invariants

  • Buyer can never access principal
  • Beneficiary is immutable after escrow
  • Atomic buy: MUSD + claim mint or revert
  • Rewards attributed and redeemed at most once

Roadmap

From proof-of-concept to live fee markets

Phase 0 — Epoch Lite (now)

Proof of rights separation

Mock NFT escrow, fixed-price MUSD sale, simulated reward settlement, principal return. Testnet demo with real ERC-20 transfers.

Phase 1 — Live source feasibility

Real veBTC adapter

Prove escrow, reward claim, and per-epoch isolation with actual Mezo veBTC positions on testnet.

Phase 2 — Experimental market

First whitelisted source

RFQ or sealed-bid auction. Real counterparties. Public data explorer and fee history.

Phase 3 — Revenue market

Cross-position, multi-source

Multiple revenue sources, forward curves, fractional claims, cross-chain adapters.

Key research gates

P0: Can a vault contract that holds veBTC isolate per-epoch rewards and return the position intact?

P1: Are per-position fee amounts large enough to justify trading and gas costs?

P2: Can sold-period votes be enforced as immutable through available interfaces?

What we're honest about

  • No real BTC deposits in Lite
  • No live Mezo fee collection yet
  • No production audit
  • Demo values are synthetic
  • Not a guaranteed-return product
